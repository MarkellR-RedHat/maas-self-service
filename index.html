Why Centralize

Pooling GPU resources eliminates shadow AI and cuts infrastructure costs.

WITHOUT MaaS

✗ N teams × N shadow deployments
✗ Wasted GPUs sitting idle per team
✗ No visibility into AI spend
✗ Compliance risk from unmanaged models
✗ Personal cloud accounts, unauthorized API keys

WITH MaaS

✓ 1 centralized stack, all teams served
✓ Pooled GPUs — higher utilization, lower cost
✓ Full cost attribution by team and model
✓ Governed access with admin-defined policy
✓ Zero shadow AI — no reason to go rogue

MaaS

Pre-deployed model endpoints for app developers. OpenAI-compatible API. Self-service keys. Governed access.

GPUaaS

Raw GPU hardware for data scientists. Training, fine-tuning, synthetic data generation. Direct hardware allocation.
